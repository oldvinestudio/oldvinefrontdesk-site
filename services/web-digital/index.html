Who owns the domain and accounts?

Your domain, Google Business Profile and analytics are set up in your name, and you keep the logins.

I build every site with clean, fast pages and the basics search engines look for, and I set up your Google Business Profile properly. Rankings depend on many things outside anyone's control, so I don't promise positions.

Yes. You can make simple edits yourself, or hand updates to me on a monthly care plan.
