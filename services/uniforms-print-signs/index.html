How it works

Four steps, in writing

See the full process

  1. Consult by email

    Tell me about your business and what you need. It's free for new businesses; established businesses usually start with the $295 Growth Audit. Everything can be handled by email. If you'd like a short call, ask and I'll offer times.

  2. Plan & quote

    I reply within one business day, and your written plan and quote follow in that reply or soon after if I need a detail from you: what I'll build, in what order, by when and for how much. Nothing starts until you say yes.

  3. Build

    I work through the plan and send each piece for your approval. Printed and embroidered items go to production only after you sign off on the proof.

  4. Launch & support

    You get your files, logins and printed items, and a short checklist for opening week. After launch, help@ is there for questions, and monthly support is available if you want it.

Straight answers

What I won't do

Print before you approve the proof

Nothing is printed, embroidered or made until you sign off on the proof.

Install signs

I design signs and prepare the files. A licensed sign contractor fabricates and installs where the job needs one.

Guess at production costs

Production is quoted for your order before anything is made, and you approve the quote first.

Questions about Uniforms, print & signs

More questions? Email me.

How are print and embroidery priced?

The design has a set price. Production depends on the item, quantity and materials, so it's arranged through print and embroidery partners and quoted for your order at cost plus handling before anything is made. You approve the quote and the proof first.

Do you install signs?

No. I design signs and prepare the files. Fabrication and installation are done by a licensed sign contractor where the job requires one.

Can I reorder later?

Yes. Your production files are kept ready so reorders match the first run.
