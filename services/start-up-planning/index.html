Questions

Questions about Start-up & planning

More questions? Email me.

Do you give legal, tax or financial advice?

No. I organize your plan, research and numbers, but the figures are your own estimates. For entity choice, taxes or contracts, talk to a CPA or an attorney. The Inland Empire SBDC and SCORE also offer free advising.

I already have a plan from the SBDC. Can you work from it?

Yes. Bring it along and I'll help you build what's in it: the name, brand, website, print and setup.

Is a name search the same as a trademark clearance?

No. It's a basic search to spot obvious conflicts. Before you file a trademark or invest heavily in a name, have an attorney run a proper clearance.
