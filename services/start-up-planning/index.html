How it works

Four steps, in writing

See the full process

  1. Consult by email

    Tell me about your business and what you need. It's free for new businesses; established businesses usually start with the $295 Growth Audit. Everything can be handled by email. If you'd like a short call, ask and I'll offer times.

  2. Plan & quote

    I reply within one business day, and your written plan and quote follow in that reply or soon after if I need a detail from you: what I'll build, in what order, by when and for how much. Nothing starts until you say yes.

  3. Build

    I work through the plan and send each piece for your approval. Printed and embroidered items go to production only after you sign off on the proof.

    You get your files, logins and printed items, and a short checklist for opening week. After launch, help@ is there for questions, and monthly support is available if you want it.

Straight answers

What I won't do

Write your numbers for you

The figures in your plan are your estimates. I organize them and show the math.

Give legal or tax advice

For entity choice, taxes or contracts, talk to a CPA or an attorney. The SBDC and SCORE advise for free.

Promise a loan or grant

A plan helps you apply. Lenders and grant programs make their own decisions.

Questions about Start-up & planning

More questions? Email me.

Do you give legal, tax or financial advice?

No. I organize your plan, research and numbers, but the figures are your own estimates. For entity choice, taxes or contracts, talk to a CPA or an attorney. The Inland Empire SBDC and SCORE also offer free advising.

I already have a plan from the SBDC. Can you work from it?

Yes. Bring it along and I'll help you build what's in it: the name, brand, website, print and setup.

Is a name search the same as a trademark clearance?

No. It's a basic search to spot obvious conflicts. Before you file a trademark or invest heavily in a name, have an attorney run a proper clearance.
