Your ad spend is separate, and it's yours.

  • You pay Google, Meta (Facebook and Instagram) or Nextdoor directly, with your own card, in an ad account in your business name.
  • My fees below cover strategy, setup, ads and management only. I never resell or mark up ad spend.
  • Suggested starting budgets for a fair 90-day test, on one platform
    • Google Search ads: $1,000-$2,000 a month (about $33-$66 a day). Clicks in home services often cost $5-$9 each.
    • Facebook and Instagram: $600-$1,500 a month (about $20-$50 a day).
    • Nextdoor: $100-$300 a month (Nextdoor suggests $3-$10 a day for local businesses).
    • Google Local Services Ads: you pay per lead, not per click. Google shows the estimated cost per lead for your trade and area during setup, before any budget is set.
  • These are starting points, not promises. Under about $1,000 a month in total, my management fee is a big share of the money, so I'll usually suggest the Ads Check-in or putting the money into your Google profile first.

Budget figures: median cost per click from the WordStream/LocaliQ 2026 Google Ads benchmarks, Meta starting budgets as reported by Stackmatix, Nextdoor's suggested daily budgets, and local-service Google budgets as reported by Redefine Web. Your own costs depend on your trade, area and season.

Who it's for

Is this a fit for your business?

  • Local service businesses that need calls now: trades, home services, cleaning, auto, beauty, wellness, food, and anyone with a clear offer and a phone that gets answered.
  • Owners already running "boosted posts" or a self-made Google Ads account who aren't sure what they're getting for the money.
  • New businesses opening soon that want an opening push while the Google profile and website build up over time.

Not a fit (yet): a business with no way to take the call or form (fix that first, maybe with the AI Front Desk), or anyone who wants guaranteed leads.

Special rules: housing, employment, credit and some health, legal and finance ads have extra platform rules and narrower targeting. I'll tell you before you pay if your category is one of them.

How it works

Four steps, in writing

  1. Plan

    Start with the $295 Ad Strategy & Account Audit, or email me your goal, area and budget. You get a written plan and quote, credited toward setup.

  2. Build in your accounts

    You create or own the ad accounts and add your card; I get manager access. I set up tracking, build the campaign and write the ads, and you approve everything before launch.

  3. Launch and watch closely

    The first weeks are a test, and I check it every week. Platforms need time and data to learn, so expect changes, not instant results.

  4. Report and decide

    Each month you see spend, leads and cost per lead next to what I changed. Keep going, change platforms, scale up or stop after the 3-month minimum.

Questions about ad campaigns

More questions? Email me.

How much should I spend on ads?

For a fair test, plan on 90 days at a level the platform can learn from: about $1,000-$2,000 a month for Google Search ads, $600-$1,500 for Facebook and Instagram, or $100-$300 for Nextdoor. Local Services Ads are priced per lead, and Google shows an estimate for your trade during setup. Start with one platform rather than spreading a small budget across three.

Is my ad budget included in your fee?

No. You pay Google, Meta or Nextdoor directly with your own card. My fee covers strategy, setup, the ads and management.

Why a flat fee instead of a percentage?

A percentage pays the manager more when you spend more, whether or not it's working. A flat fee up to $5,000 a month in spend keeps the advice about your results. Above that, the extra 10% covers the extra work of a larger account.

Which platform should I start with?

If people search for what you do ("plumber near me", "mobile detailing Temecula"), usually Google Search or Local Services Ads. If people buy it when they see it (food, beauty, events, local shops), usually Facebook and Instagram. Nextdoor fits home services that live on neighbor recommendations. The $295 plan answers this for your business specifically.

Can you guarantee leads?

No. I can promise the setup, tracking, weekly checks and honest reporting in your quote. If the numbers say ads aren't paying off for you, I'll tell you and suggest where the money would do more.

Who owns the ad account if I stop managing it?

You do, always. It's in your business name with your payment card. You remove my access and keep every campaign, ad and report.

Can you show me results for other clients?

Old Vine Studio is new, so I don't have client results yet, and I won't make any up. You'll get a written plan before you pay anything beyond the $295 audit, and you'll see the platform's own numbers every month.

What do you need from me?

A clear offer, your service area, the ad account set up in your name with your card (I'll walk you through it), someone answering the phone or forms quickly, and about 20 minutes a month to review the report.
