Questions about Growth

More questions? Email me.

Do you guarantee more customers?

No. Anyone who guarantees sales or rankings should make you nervous. I fix what's in your control, explain what usually helps and report honestly on what was done.

Can I start with just an audit?

Yes. The Growth Audit is $295: a written report you keep, plus a 30-minute call to walk through it. Act on it yourself or ask me to quote the fixes. If you book any package within 30 days, the $295 is credited in full.

Is the monthly plan a contract?

The Monthly Growth Plan has a 3-month minimum, then continues month to month. Cancel with 30 days' notice by email.
