Who it's for

Is this a fit for your business?

  • New businesses that need a logo and the first round of printed and digital pieces to match.
  • Established businesses whose flyers, menus, signs and social posts all look a little different.
  • Owners running ads or events who need designs sized correctly for each platform and printer.

Not a fit: copies of another company's logo or look, characters or brand names you don't own on merch, or "just one free concept first".

Straight answers

What I won't do

No copying

I won't copy another company's logo, layout or look, or put characters, sports logos or other brands on your shirts and merch. It's someone else's property, and it can get your business in trouble.

No stock logos sold as custom

Your logo is designed for your business, not pulled from a template site.

Your files are yours

Once the project is paid, you get the editable source files where the service includes them, plus print-ready files, and I sign over my rights in the final design. No "pay me again to get your logo file".

No surprise print charges

Printing, garments and sign fabrication are quoted per order before anything is made, and you approve the proof first.

No unlimited-revision promises I can't keep

Each service lists its rounds. Extra rounds are $95, and I tell you before starting one.

No long contracts

Design is per project or per hour. There's nothing to cancel.

Questions about graphic design

More questions? Email me.

Do I own the designs?

Yes. Once the project is paid in full, you get the final files (vector and print-ready, plus editable source files where the service includes them) and a written transfer of my rights in the final design.

What files will I get?

Vector files (SVG, PDF, and Adobe Illustrator or EPS where useful) for print and signs, PNG and JPG for web and social, and editable files for templates and decks. Every file is named by use and size.

Can you match my existing logo and colors?

Yes. Send what you have. If your logo only exists as a blurry image, I'll quote rebuilding it as a clean vector file before anything else, because signs and shirts need one.

Can you handle the printing?

Yes, if you want. Printing, garments and sign production are quoted per order at cost plus handling, and you approve the final proof before anything is ordered. You can also take the files to any printer you like.

How fast can you turn around a flyer or ad?

Flyers and door hangers take 3 business days to the first draft, ad packs 5, and decks 7. Each service shows its turnaround. If you have an event date, put it in your first email.

Can you show me design work for other clients?

Old Vine Studio is new, so there are no client projects to show yet. The Work page shows the studio's own brand work, clearly labeled as samples, and nothing there is presented as client work.

Can you put a sports team, cartoon character or another brand on our shirts?

No. Those belong to other companies, and printing them without a license can bring takedowns or legal trouble. I'll design something original that fits your event instead.
