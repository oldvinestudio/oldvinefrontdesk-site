Who it's for

Is this a fit for your business?

  • Local service businesses (trades, home services, wellness, beauty, auto, food) that serve the Temecula Valley and want more of the calls that start with a Google search.
  • Owners whose website is live but quiet: few calls or forms, and they don't know whether the site is even showing up.
  • Businesses with a messy online footprint: an old address or phone number on directories, duplicate Google profiles, or a site rebuilt without anyone checking the search basics.

Not a fit: anyone who wants guaranteed rankings, ranking in cities where they don't actually work, fake reviews, or bought links. I'll say no to those (what I won't do).

Questions about local SEO

More questions? Email me.

How long until I see results?

It depends on your site, your competition and how much needs fixing. Google says some changes take effect in a few hours and others take several months, and suggests waiting a few weeks before judging any change. That's why the monthly plan has a 3-month minimum, not a 12-month one.

Can you guarantee I'll rank on the first page?

No, and you should be wary of anyone who does. Google's guidance on hiring an SEO says no one can guarantee top placement. What I can promise is the work on your quote, done on the dates on your quote, with every change shown to you.

Do I need SEO if I already have a Google Business Profile?

The profile is the biggest single piece for most local businesses, so if yours isn't set up, start with Google Business Profile Setup ($295). SEO work adds the website and listings that back the profile up.

What access do you need?

Read access to Google Search Console to start, manager access to your Google profile, and an editor login for your website if you want me to make the fixes. You keep ownership of all of them, and you can remove my access any time.

Can you show me results for other clients?

Old Vine Studio is new, so I don't have client results to show yet, and I won't invent any. Instead you'll see a written plan before you pay, and a change list and Google's own numbers every month after.

Will you write pages for every town I serve?

Only if each page has something real and different to say about that town: the jobs you do there, local rules, and photos from there. Pages that just swap the city name are what Google's spam policies call doorway pages, and they can hurt more than help.

Do you buy links or reviews?

No. Both break Google's rules, and fake or bought reviews also break the FTC's rule on consumer reviews. I'll help you ask real customers for honest reviews with the Review Request System.

What happens if I cancel the monthly plan?

After the 3-month minimum, give 30 days' notice by email. Everything I made is already in your accounts, and I'll send a short handover note on what to keep doing.
