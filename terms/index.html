Quotes and payment

  • Every project starts with a written quote showing the price, what's included, revision rounds and dates. Nothing starts until you approve it.
  • Projects: 50% to start and 50% before launch or before final files are handed over. Services under $500 are paid in full to start.
  • Printing, embroidery and signs are arranged through print partners and quoted per order at cost plus handling. They're paid in full when you approve the proof, before the order is placed.
  • Monthly plans are billed monthly in advance. Website Care is month to month. Social Media Management and the Monthly Growth Plan have a 3-month minimum and then continue month to month. Cancel with 30 days' notice by email. The first month of the Monthly Growth Plan included in Grow doesn't commit you to more: the 3-month minimum only starts if you choose to continue.
  • Invoices are due within 7 days. Payment details come with your invoice.
  • You own your final files once the project is paid in full. Your domain, Google profile and social accounts are set up in your name from day one.
  • If a project waits 30 days on content or feedback from you, I'll invoice the work done so far, and we can pick it back up when you're ready.
  • Software, domain, hosting and email subscriptions are paid by you, directly to those providers.

Refunds

  • Before work starts: full refund of anything you've paid.
  • After work starts: I keep the deposit (50% of the project price) for the work begun, and I refund anything you've paid beyond that for work not yet delivered.
  • Printed, embroidered and other made-to-order items can't be refunded once they're ordered, because they're made for you. Please check your proof carefully: it's what gets made.
  • Monthly plans: cancel with 30 days' notice by email, after the 3-month minimum where a plan has one.
  • AI Front Desk has its own billing and refund terms on its policies page.

Revisions and timing

  • Each service shows the revision rounds it includes. Most design and website work includes 2, and written work such as plans and reports includes 1.
  • A round is one combined list of changes from the person making the final decision, sent within 5 business days of getting a draft. I send each revised version within 3 business days.
  • Extra rounds are $95 each, and I'll tell you before starting one. Unused rounds don't carry over to other items.
  • A new direction after you've approved a concept, or anything not in your quote, is new work. I'll quote it in writing first.
  • Once you approve a print proof, any changes mean a new print order.
  • Turnaround times are business days to your first draft, counted from when your payment and the content I need (logo files, photos, text, logins) arrive. If feedback takes longer than 5 business days, the schedule moves by the same amount. Printing, embroidery and shipping add partner production time, which I'll tell you before you order.

Your files and accounts

  • Once your project is paid in full, you own your final files, delivered in the formats you need.
  • Your domain, Google profile and social accounts are set up in your name from day one, and you keep the logins.
  • Software, domain, hosting and email subscriptions are paid by you, directly to those providers.

Monthly plan minimums

  • Website Care is month to month. Social Media Management and the Monthly Growth Plan have a 3-month minimum and then continue month to month. See monthly plans.
  • Monthly plans: cancel with 30 days' notice by email, after the 3-month minimum where a plan has one.
