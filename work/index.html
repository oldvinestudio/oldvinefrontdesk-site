Sample project: Old Vine Studio's own brand

Old Vine Studio brand system

The job
Turn the studio's logo, a lime-green circuit-vine growing through the "old vine studio" wordmark, into a complete set of files that works everywhere a small business shows up: a browser tab, a social profile, a cover banner and print.
What I did
Prepared the logo on a transparent background in two versions, one with dark text for light backgrounds and one with light text for dark backgrounds. Cut a standalone vine mark, made favicons at 32 and 180 pixels on navy and light backgrounds, a square 1080-pixel profile image and a 1640 x 624 Facebook cover.
Why it matters for you
A logo is only useful if you have the right file for each job. Every brand project ends with this kind of file set, named so you can find what you need.
Delivered
Transparent logo (light and dark text), vine mark, favicons, profile image and cover banner.
Sample project: Old Vine Studio's own launch

AI Front Desk social launch kit

The job
Launch a brand-new service on social media from zero followers, with no paid ads and a few hours a week of the owner's time.
What I did
Wrote profile names, categories and bios for seven platforms, ranked by how useful each is likely to be for local leads. Built a two-week post plan (14 daily posts plus 6 short-video scripts) and a 7-day launch calendar with captions and alt text, along with 9 feed images, 3 story images, a profile image and a cover. Also wrote a weekly time budget so the plan stays realistic.
Rules built in
No prices in posts, no invented stats or testimonials, and no suggestion that a person answers the AI line.
Delivered
A ready-to-post kit the owner can work through himself, one day at a time.
