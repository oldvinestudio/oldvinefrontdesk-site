    Questions

    What owners ask us first

    Will callers know it's not a person?

    Yes. It introduces itself as your business's AI assistant at the start of every call, speaks naturally and keeps the call short. Many callers care that somebody picked up and got their request to you.

    Does it block spam calls?

    It screens every call your receptionist answers (the calls you forward to it). Spam and sales calls are politely ended and never sent to you; real customers always come through.

    Is this only for big trade companies?

    No. It's built for home and local service businesses of any size, especially owner-operators who are on a job, under a car or up a ladder when the phone rings. Detailers, landscapers, pool techs, cleaners, handymen, locksmiths, groomers and more all get the same plans and prices.

    What if it gets something wrong?

    You get a summary of every call, and we read the transcripts each week and fix the script. It's set up to pass anything outside its script to you as a request or a transfer, rather than make up an answer.

    Do I need a new phone number?

    No. You keep your number and forward calls to the receptionist, usually only when you're busy or don't pick up. Turning it off takes one setting on your phone.

    What happens with an emergency call?

    You decide what counts as an emergency. Those calls go straight to your on-call phone, and if nobody answers, the receptionist takes the details, marks them urgent and sends an alert to your phone right away.

    Who decides the appointment time?

    You do. By default every call becomes a job request sent to your phone with the details and the caller's preferred times, and your team confirms the time. If you like, it can offer only the windows you open, and those still come to you to approve. Pause it, change hours or block a service any time by emailing us.

    Is there a contract?

    No. Plans are month to month and you can cancel any time. You keep your own business number, so stopping is one forwarding setting on your phone.

    Who is behind Old Vine Studio?

    Colton, based in Temecula. The person who sets up your receptionist is the same person you email when you want something changed, and you get an answer within one business day.

    Billing, refunds, terms and privacy

    Plain-language rules for working with Old Vine Studio. Updated November 2, 2026. Text messages are covered by our SMS terms.

    Billing and cancellation

    Plans are billed monthly in advance by card through Stripe, our payment processor. The Founding plan charges $99 at sign-up, which covers your first 30 days live: the 30 days start the day your receptionist goes live, not the day you pay. After that it is $149 a month. Standard charges a $299 one-time setup fee and $199 a month. Busy is $299 a month, with no new setup fee when you move up from Founding or Standard. The price you sign up at stays the same for as long as you stay subscribed, unless you choose to change plans. Cancel any time from the billing link in your receipt or by emailing OldVineStudio@gmail.com; you won't be charged again, and service runs to the end of the period you paid for. The phone line and receptionist software are included in your plan; there is no separate software bill.

    Call minutes

    We host your receptionist and pay for its call minutes. Founding and Standard include 500 minutes a month (about 150–250 calls), Busy includes 1,500, and above that we quote a custom plan. If you go over in one month, nothing changes and nothing is charged; we send you a friendly heads-up with your minutes. If you go over two months in a row, you choose: move up a plan, or we help you narrow what gets forwarded (for example, after-hours only). Calls are never cut off mid-month and there are no surprise charges. Your plan price never changes unless you choose to upgrade. If usage stays above double your allowance (1,000 minutes on Founding or Standard) for two months in a row and you don’t want to upgrade or narrow forwarding, we may end the plan with 30 days’ notice and no further charges.

    Refunds

    If your receptionist isn't live within 10 business days of receiving your setup answers, we refund your first payment in full. Otherwise, payments already made aren't refunded, but you can cancel at any time and won't be billed again. If you think you were charged by mistake, email us and we'll fix it within two business days.
