How it works

Four steps from first email to launch day

One local studio and one point of contact, with the scope and dates in writing.

See the full process

  1. Consult by email

    Tell me about your business and what you need. It's free for new businesses; established businesses usually start with the $295 Growth Audit. Everything can be handled by email. If you'd like a short call, ask and I'll offer times.

    Local and email-first. I'm based in Temecula, and you can do the whole project on your own schedule.

  2. Plan & quote

    I reply within one business day, and your written plan and quote follow in that reply or soon after if I need a detail from you: what I'll build, in what order, by when and for how much. Nothing starts until you say yes.

    Honest scope. What's included, what isn't, revision rounds and dates are all in writing.

  3. Build

    I work through the plan and send each piece for your approval. Printed and embroidered items go to production only after you sign off on the proof.

    Everything in one place. Plan, brand, website and print are built to match, so you don't chase five vendors.

  4. Launch & support

    You get your files, logins and printed items, and a short checklist for opening week. After launch, help@ is there for questions, and monthly support is available if you want it.

    One point of contact. You work with me, Colton, from the first email to launch day and after.

What owners ask first

More answers on How I work. Anything else? Email a question.

How much does it cost?

Packages are $2,450 (Launch), $2,950 (Brand) and $3,450 (Grow). Single services start at $95, and monthly help starts at $59 a month. Every price is on the Services and Packages pages. Printing, embroidery and sign production depend on quantity and materials, so they're quoted for your order before anything is made. Your written quote is the price you pay unless you add to the scope.

How long does it take?

It depends on what you need. Each service shows a typical turnaround in business days, and packages take about 5 to 6 weeks. Your quote has a target date for every step. Printed and embroidered items add partner production and shipping time, which I'll tell you about before you order.

How many revisions do I get?

Most design and website work includes 2 revision rounds, and written work such as plans and reports includes 1. Each service shows its number. Extra rounds are $95 each, and I'll always tell you before starting one.

Do I own my logo and website?

Yes. Once your project is paid in full, you own your final files, delivered in the formats you need. Fonts and any stock images keep their own licenses, and I'll tell you which ones you're using. Your domain, Google Business Profile and social accounts are set up in your name from day one, and you keep the logins.

Do you guarantee results?

No, and anyone who does should make you nervous. I'll build you a clear brand, a working website and a solid setup, and I'll be honest about what usually helps. Sales, calls and search rankings depend on your market, your offer and many things outside my control. Results vary.

Can we talk on the phone?

Everything can be handled by email, so you have it all in writing. If you'd like a short call, ask and I'll offer times. There's no studio phone line: the only number on this site is the AI Front Desk demo line, where an AI receptionist answers.

Tell me what you're building.

Send one email. I reply within one business day, Monday to Friday, usually with a written plan and quote.
