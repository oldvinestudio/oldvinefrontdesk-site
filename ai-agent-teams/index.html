How it works

From your week to a working team

  1. Tell me about your week

    A short email questionnaire about where your admin time goes and which tools you use (calendar, Jobber, QuickBooks, Square, Google Sheets). If you'd like a short call instead, ask and I'll offer times.

  2. I build your team

    I set up each agent with your prices, services, rules and tone, and test it on real examples from your business before you use it.

  3. Your team in an app on your phone

    That's what you get at launch: ask an agent for a quote draft, check tomorrow's jobs, or clear your approval inbox in a few taps.

  4. You approve what goes out

    Emails, texts, bookings, review replies and posts wait for your OK. The one exception is the Website Question Helper, which answers website visitors only from answers you've approved. Agents aren't given access to charge cards, give refunds or change your prices.

  5. I keep it working

    Small edits are included every month. New agents, new tools or more usage are priced up front, and nothing changes until you say yes.

Example agents

What your agents can do

Each agent does one job, with your prices, rules and tone. Pick the ones that fit your business.

Quote Drafter

Turns your job notes or a photo into a line-item estimate using your own price sheet. It flags anything that isn't on the sheet. You review and send.

Scheduling Assistant

Reads new job requests and suggests open times based on your calendar and your rules. You pick one, and only then does it go on the calendar.

Lead Follow-up

Spots quotes that haven't had a reply and drafts a short, polite follow-up in your voice. It stops when you tell it to, and it honors opt-outs.

Review Reply Drafter

Drafts replies to new Google reviews: thank-yous for happy customers, a calm "please call me" for unhappy ones. You post them.

Daily Job Briefing

Every evening: tomorrow's jobs, addresses in order, notes and a materials list, all on one screen.

Website Question Helper

Answers customer questions on your site using only the answers you've approved. It tells visitors up front that it's an AI assistant and passes anything else to you.

More agents: invoice reminders, bookkeeping prep, weekly owner report, maintenance reminders, materials lists, event inquiries, an internal handbook Q&A.

The app

Your team in an app on your phone

What you get at launch (there is nothing to download before your team is set up):

  • My Team: a card for each agent with what it does, whether it's working or paused, and a pause switch.
  • Approval inbox: every email, text, booking, review reply or post an agent drafts, with Approve, Edit or Reject. Website Question Helper chats don't go through it: it answers visitors only from answers you've approved.
  • Usage meter: how many of this month's actions you've used and when the count resets.
  • Requests: ask for a change, a new agent or more usage, with the price shown before you send it.

What's an action?

One finished job by one agent: one quote draft, one scheduling suggestion, one review reply, one answer in the chat. Bigger "deep" jobs, like a month-end summary, use 5 actions, and the app tells you before it starts.

When you reach your limit, your agents pause

You're never billed extra automatically. You get a heads-up at 80%. If you want more, extra actions are priced up front: 250 for $49 or 1,000 for $199 this month, or +500 every month for $99. Nothing is added unless you ask for it and I approve the request, and the extra actions start once that invoice is paid.

Add-ons

An extra agent from the list is $250 setup + $49/month; a custom agent is $600 setup + $79/month, scoped in writing first. A new connected tool or a bigger change to an agent is quoted in writing before any work starts.

Terms

50% of setup at signing, 50% at go-live. 3-month minimum, then month to month with 30 days' notice. Billed monthly through Stripe, with each invoice emailed to you.

Refunds and cancelling

Full refund before the build starts. After the build starts, the 50% setup deposit is kept and any unearned balance is refunded. Monthly fees aren't refunded; after the 3-month minimum, cancel with 30 days' notice.

Questions

Questions about AI Agent Teams

More questions? Email me.

Is anyone on the team a person?

No. The team is AI agents, which are software. I'm the one person behind it: I build the agents, tune them and handle your requests. Anything an agent drafts for a customer waits for you to approve and send. The one exception is the Website Question Helper, which answers website visitors only from answers you've approved.

Do the agents send emails or book jobs on their own?

No. Emails, texts, bookings and posts become a card in your approval inbox. You read it, edit it if you like, and tap Approve. The one exception is the Website Question Helper, which answers website visitors only from answers you've approved. Agents aren't given access to charge cards, give refunds or change your prices.

Will my customers know they're talking to AI?

Yes. Only the Website Question Helper talks to customers directly, and its first message says it's your business's AI assistant. If someone asks, it says so again and offers a way to reach you. Everything else is a draft that you send yourself.

What happens if an agent gets something wrong?

Drafts come to you before they go anywhere, because emails, texts, bookings and posts need your approval. The Website Question Helper answers only from answers you've approved and passes anything else to you. Tap "Report a problem" on any answer and it comes to me, and fixing it doesn't count against your included edits. Quote totals are calculated from your price sheet, not guessed, and anything that isn't on the sheet is flagged for you.

Can my bill go up without me knowing?

No. Your plan has a fixed monthly price and a hard cap on actions. At 100% your agents pause until your next cycle. Extra usage, new agents and changes are always priced before you ask for them, and nothing is added until you approve and pay that invoice.

What happens to my business data?

It's used only to run your agents. The agents run on xAI's Grok models. xAI's API security FAQ says it doesn't train on your API inputs or outputs without your explicit permission, and it stores requests for 30 days for auditing in case of suspected abuse or misuse, then deletes them. I never sell your data. You can ask for a full export or deletion at any time, by email or in the app. I don't take medical or patient data.

Which tools do the agents work with?

Google Calendar, Google Sheets and Square to start. Jobber, QuickBooks Online and Google Business Profile connections can be requested; each needs that company's app approval, which I apply for. If you use something else, ask. I'll tell you honestly whether it can connect.

Can I add an agent or change one later?

Yes. Ask by email, or use "Request a change" or "Request a new agent" in the app. The price shows before you send it, and I take it from there. Small edits like wording, a new FAQ answer or a schedule rule are included each month.

Who builds and looks after it?

Me, Colton, in Temecula. The person who builds your team is the same person you email when you want something changed: quote@oldvinefrontdesk.com for new work.
