Hi, I'm Colton. I run Old Vine Studio from Temecula. You'll work with me directly, by email, from your first message to launch day and after.

The steps

Four steps, in writing

  1. Consult by email

    Tell me about your business and what you need. It's free for new businesses; established businesses usually start with the $295 Growth Audit. Everything can be handled by email. If you'd like a short call, ask and I'll offer times.

    Local and email-first. I'm based in Temecula, and you can do the whole project on your own schedule.

  2. Plan & quote

    I reply within one business day, and your written plan and quote follow in that reply or soon after if I need a detail from you: what I'll build, in what order, by when and for how much. Nothing starts until you say yes.

    Honest scope. What's included, what isn't, revision rounds and dates are all in writing.

  3. Build

    I work through the plan and send each piece for your approval. Printed and embroidered items go to production only after you sign off on the proof.

    Everything in one place. Plan, brand, website and print are built to match, so you don't chase five vendors.

  4. Launch & support

    You get your files, logins and printed items, and a short checklist for opening week. After launch, help@ is there for questions, and monthly support is available if you want it.

    One point of contact. You work with me, Colton, from the first email to launch day and after.

Questionnaires

Some services start with a short written questionnaire, so your details are in writing before I begin. You only answer the one for the service you bought:

  • Launch Roadmap: a written intake questionnaire about your idea, budget and timing.
  • Logo and brand work: a short questionnaire about your style and customers.
  • AI Front Desk: 10 short questions about your services, area, hours and rules.

How I communicate

  • Email is the main channel, so you have everything in writing.
  • I reply within one business day, Monday to Friday.
  • Everything can be handled by email. If you'd like a short call, ask and I'll offer times.
  • Each piece comes to you for approval, and printed and embroidered items go to production only after you sign off on the proof.

If a draft isn't right

Nothing here is a guarantee of results. It's how changes work:

  1. Send one combined list of changes from the person making the final decision, within 5 business days of getting the draft. "Doesn't feel like us" is a fine place to start.
  2. I send each revised version within 3 business days.
  3. Each service lists its revision rounds. Extra rounds are $95 each, and I'll tell you before starting one. A new direction after you've approved a concept is new work, and I'll quote it in writing first.
  4. If you decide to stop, the refund rules apply.

Payment

  • Projects: 50% to start and 50% before launch or before final files are handed over. Services under $500 are paid in full to start.
  • Payment details come with your invoice. Invoices are due within 7 days.
  • Full details: client terms.

More questions

Anything else? Email a question.

What does your business consulting include?

Practical help with launching and growing a small business: a lean business plan built on your own numbers, market research, pricing, a name shortlist, then the brand, website, Google Business Profile, print and setup that follow from it. I don't give legal, tax or financial advice, and I'll point you to the free advisers at the SBDC and SCORE when you need them. The business plan and startup help page lists each service and price.

What if I don't have a business plan yet?

That's fine, and it's common. I can help you put together a lean plan: your customer, competitors, pricing and a first-steps checklist, using your own numbers and goals. The free advisers at the Inland Empire SBDC, SCORE and Temecula's TVE2 are also worth using. I don't give legal, tax or financial advice.

How does printing work?

I design the item and send you a proof. Once you approve it, printing is arranged through a print or embroidery partner and the order is shipped to you. Production is quoted per order at cost plus handling, and sign installation is done by a licensed sign contractor where the job needs one.

Do you work outside Temecula?

My home base is the Temecula Valley: Temecula, Murrieta, Menifee, Lake Elsinore and Wildomar. Most design, web and social work happens by email, so I can often help businesses farther away too. Ask in your quote request.

Can we talk on the phone?

Everything can be handled by email, so you have it all in writing. If you'd like a short call, ask and I'll offer times. There's no studio phone line: the only number on this site is the AI Front Desk demo line, where an AI receptionist answers.
