Privacy policy

Privacy, in plain language

What this website collects, why, and the choices you have. Last updated November 18, 2026.

Who we are

Old Vine Studio is a design, website, print and marketing studio based in Temecula, California, run by Colton. This policy covers this website (oldvinefrontdesk.com) and email you send us. The AI Front Desk service has its own privacy and text-message terms on the AI Front Desk page.

What we collect

Email you send us. When you email quote@, info@ or help@, we receive your name, email address, what you write and any files you attach. The quote form on the contact page doesn't send anything by itself: it fills in an email in your own email app, and nothing reaches us until you press send there.

Analytics, only if you accept. If you click Accept on the notice, this site uses Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which pages and buttons are used. Google Analytics sets cookies and collects things like pages viewed, button clicks (for example "Get a quote"), device and browser type, and an approximate location derived from your IP address. If you click Decline, or your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, Google Analytics does not load, and if you change your choice to Decline, its cookies are removed from this site.

Basic server logs. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical data such as IP addresses to keep the service secure. The live preview and the unsubscribe page load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address; the other pages use fonts stored on this site.

How we use it

To answer your questions, prepare quotes, deliver projects and support you afterwards, and, if you accepted analytics, to understand which pages help visitors so we can improve the site. We do not use your information for advertising.

We don't sell your data

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share it only with the providers that run our services: Google (email and, if you accept, Analytics), GitHub (website hosting), and production partners when you order printed or embroidered items (only the details needed to make and ship your order).

How long we keep it

Emails and project records are kept as long as needed to serve you and to meet tax and record-keeping rules. Google Analytics data is kept for the retention period set in our Analytics account, no longer than 14 months.

Your choices

You can accept or decline analytics at any time: . You can also block cookies in your browser. To see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, email us.

California privacy notice

If you live in California, the California Consumer Privacy Act gives you the right to know what personal information we collect and how we use it, to get a copy, to ask us to correct or delete it, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not collect sensitive personal information through this website. We treat a Global Privacy Control signal as a request to opt out. We will not treat you differently for using these rights. To make a request, email info@oldvinefrontdesk.com with the subject "Privacy request"; we may need to confirm your identity first. An authorized agent may make a request for you with your written permission.

Children

This site is for business owners and is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact
