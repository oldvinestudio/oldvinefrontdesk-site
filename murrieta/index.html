Opening in Murrieta

Local steps for a Murrieta business

Each step links to the official city or county page, so you can check the current rules and fees yourself. General information, not legal advice.

  1. City business license

    Every person or company planning to do business within Murrieta city limits applies for a City of Murrieta business license. The application is online and open around the clock, and if your office is inside the city you attach the required documents when you apply. The city publishes its fee schedule as a PDF. Murrieta business licenses

  2. Running it from home

    Murrieta has a separate home occupation permit form. Read it before you open so you know the conditions for working from home. Home occupation permit (PDF)

  3. Free advice and local networking

    SCORE Inland Empire offers free mentoring and workshops. The Chamber of Southwest Riverside County is based at the Murrieta Innovation Center. SCORE Inland Empire · Chamber of Southwest Riverside County

  4. Your business name

    If you'll trade under a name that isn't your own legal name, file a fictitious business name statement with Riverside County: $66 for the first name and owner, then four weeks of newspaper notices. The Riverside County DBA guide walks through it. Riverside County fictitious business names

  5. Where I fit

    The license, name filing and advice are yours to do, and the launch checklist lists every step. I'm a short drive down the 15, and I handle what customers see: a name and logo that fit, cards, shirts, a website and a Google profile that's filled out properly.
