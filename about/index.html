My story

Hi, I'm Colton.

I run Old Vine Studio from Temecula. It started with design work and the AI Front Desk, an AI phone receptionist I set up for home and local service businesses so the calls they forward get answered while they're on a job.

Working on that showed me how much a new owner has to juggle. A phone that gets answered helps, but you also need a name people remember, a logo that looks right on a shirt, cards, a website, a Google profile and a sign. It's easy to end up coordinating five vendors, or doing it all at midnight.

So Old Vine Studio grew into a launch and growth studio: one local place that takes you from idea to open doors, and helps you keep growing after that.

Mission

Old Vine Studio helps Temecula Valley owners go from idea to open doors, with one local studio handling the plan, the brand, the website, the print and the phone.

What I do

From the plan to the polo

I help Temecula Valley owners start and grow their businesses. That covers planning and market research, naming, logos and brand kits, websites and Google profiles, social media setup, business cards, uniforms, banners and sign designs, and the AI Front Desk. Physical items are designed here, with printing arranged through print and embroidery partners.

Who I serve

  • People starting from nothing who want a business that looks established on day one.
  • Owner-operators (trades, home services, food, beauty, retail, wellness and more) who are busy doing the work and short on time for marketing.
  • Existing businesses ready for a rebrand, a better website or help answering the calls you forward.

Service area

Temecula, Murrieta, Menifee, Lake Elsinore and Wildomar, with email-based work for businesses farther away.
