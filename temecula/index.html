Opening in Temecula

Local steps for a Temecula business

Each step links to the official city or county page, so you can check the current rules and fees yourself. General information, not legal advice.

  1. City business license

    Anyone doing business inside Temecula city limits needs a City of Temecula business license. The city lists $39 for most businesses and $59 for home-based ones, you can apply online or by mail, and every license expires each January 31. Temecula business license instructions

  2. Running it from home

    Home-based businesses file the city's Home Occupation form with the license application. It carries a one-time $20 fee, which is part of the $59.

  3. Free advice nearby

    TVE2 and the city's Entrepreneurial Resource Center on Business Park Drive offer no-cost consulting with advisers from partners such as the Small Business Development Center, plus workshops and an incubator for qualified startups. TVE2 · Entrepreneurial Resource Center

  4. Your business name

    If you'll trade under a name that isn't your own legal name, file a fictitious business name statement with Riverside County: $66 for the first name and owner, then four weeks of newspaper notices. The Riverside County DBA guide walks through it. Riverside County fictitious business names

  5. Where I fit

    The license, name filing and advice are yours to do, and how to start a business in Temecula lists every step and fee. I handle the parts customers see: the logo, cards, website, Google profile and the signs on your door, so the business you register looks ready on the day it opens.

How much is a business license in Temecula?

The City of Temecula lists $39 for a commercial business license ($35 registration plus a $4 state fee) and $59 for a home-based business, which adds a one-time $20 home occupation fee. Licenses expire each January 31. Check the city's page for current fees before you apply.

Do I need a permit to run a business from home in Temecula?

Yes. Home-based businesses file the city's Home Occupation form along with the business license application. The full steps are in how to start a business in Temecula.
