Opening in Menifee

Local steps for a Menifee business

Each step links to the official city or county page, so you can check the current rules and fees yourself. General information, not legal advice.

  1. City business license

    Menifee's business license FAQ says all businesses operating or doing business within city limits need a City of Menifee business license. For July 2025 to June 2026 the city lists $80.49 plus a $4 CASP fee for a new license, prorated by start date, and every license expires December 31. Menifee business license FAQs

  2. Check your zoning first

    Confirm with the city's Planning Department that your business use is allowed in your zone. In-city businesses also pay a $43.42 zoning review fee, and commercial spaces need an approved Certificate of Occupancy from Building and Safety.

  3. City business resources

    Menifee's economic development site lists start-up assistance, business workshops and consulting, a zoning check tool and SizeUp Menifee market data. MenifeeBusiness.com business resources

  4. Your business name

    If you'll trade under a name that isn't your own legal name, file a fictitious business name statement with Riverside County: $66 for the first name and owner, then four weeks of newspaper notices. The Riverside County DBA guide walks through it. Riverside County fictitious business names

  5. Where I fit

    The license, zoning check and name filing are yours to do, and the launch checklist lists every step. I handle the pieces customers see: the name and logo, cards, a website and a Google profile with the right service area.

How much is a Menifee business license?

For July 1, 2025 to June 30, 2026, Menifee lists $80.49 plus a $4 CASP fee for a new license, with lower prorated amounts for businesses that start later in the year. Businesses inside the city also pay a $43.42 zoning review fee. Check the city's FAQ for current amounts.

When does a Menifee business license expire?

Every Menifee business license runs January 1 to December 31, so renewals are due by December 31 each year. New businesses get a license period adjusted to their start date.
