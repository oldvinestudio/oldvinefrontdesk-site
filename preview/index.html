Questions owners ask

The short answers.

Will it book jobs or promise times without me?

No. By default it takes the request and the caller's preferred times, and your team confirms. If you turn on "offer windows I open", it only offers the windows you list, and you still approve each one.

Will callers know it's AI?

Yes. The greeting says it's your business's AI assistant and that the call is recorded. If anyone asks again, it tells them the same.

Does it screen spam and sales calls?

Yes, free on every plan. Robocalls, scam calls and sales pitches are ended politely and never become alerts on your phone; they only show up as a count in your morning summary. Screening applies to calls your receptionist answers (the calls you forward to it).

What if it gets something wrong?

You get the transcript of every call. Email us what to change and we update the script, usually the same day.

Do I need a new phone number?

No. You forward busy and no-answer calls from your current number, so you and your team still answer first.

How do I cancel?

Reply "cancel" to any email, or use the billing link in your Stripe receipt. Service runs to the end of the period you paid for, and you're not charged again. Turn off call forwarding and it's done; your business number stays yours.
